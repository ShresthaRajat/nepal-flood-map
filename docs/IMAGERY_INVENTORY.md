# Imagery Inventory — Nepal Flood 2026 (Bhote Koshi / Trishuli, 26 Aug 2026)

Purpose: a single inspectable inventory of every publicly discoverable satellite, drone, and derived-product image or dataset related to the 26 Aug 2026 glacier-collapse flood on the Lhende/Bhote Koshi/Trishuli rivers (Rasuwa & Nuwakot districts, Nepal / Kyirong, Tibet), merged from three separate research passes (commercial open-data programmes, space agencies & international mechanisms, and news/social/OSINT/drone sources), so that Rajat can inspect what exists, decide what to add to the map, and cite sources later.

**Compiled:** 7 Sep 2026. All links below were verified to respond (not necessarily to serve a full download — see per-row notes) as of that date.

**Update 9 Sep 2026:** the Vantor Nepal-Flooding-Aug-2026 STAC collection now has 30 items (was 23 on 7 Sep). Three of the new 8 Sep scenes — WV-2 `B030001100EF5210`, WV-2 `B030001100EF5110`, Legion `B14000110116A310` — failed `tools/imagery_watch.py`'s auto-build cloud/off-nadir gates and were left notify-only; the owner added them to the map by hand anyway (see the three rows below marked ON MAP, added 9 Sep 2026).

**Status legend:**
- **ON MAP** — this exact scene/date is already on the Nepal flood 2026 map.
- **NEW – downloadable georeferenced** — not yet on the map; a real file (GeoTIFF/COG/shapefile/etc.) can be pulled directly.
- **NEW – viewer or quicklook only** — not yet on the map; only a web viewer, dashboard, or quicklook/thumbnail is available, no bulk direct-download link found.
- **PRESS IMAGE ONLY** — editorial/news image with no georeferenced file or public dataset behind it.
- **NOTHING FOUND** — provider/source actively checked; no event-specific product located.

The map already has: Sentinel-2 2026-06-03, 2026-08-27; Sentinel-1 2026-08-16, 2026-08-28; PlanetScope 2026-05-27, 08-26, 08-28; SkySat 08-27, 08-31; Pelican 09-01; Vantor WV-2 08-28, WV-3 08-27, Legion 02-05, 09-01, 09-07 (two strips); one unconfirmed social-media drone image.

No direct download page found; follow up via ASF DAAC/NASA Earthdata Search. https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/nasa-and-isros-nisar-detected-warnings-weeks-before-nepal-tibet-avalanche/tldr
