# Gauge-based flood-front timeline (reference)

**Status: NOT wired into the map or animation yet.** This is source material for
a future map animation only.

> **All times below are approximate** (rounded to the reporting granularity used
> by the sources, mostly the nearest minute, some ranges to the nearest 5–10
> minutes). **Before showing any part of this timeline on the actual map,
> obtain and cite the primary source: the Flood Forecasting Division (FFD)
> technical report of 27 August 2026**, prepared by the Centre of Hydrology and
> Water Resources Research, Nepal. Everything here is transcribed from a
> Wikipedia summary of that report plus contemporary news coverage, not the
> report itself — treat times and readings as provisional until checked against
> the FFD document.

## 26 August 2026 — gauge and flood-front timeline

| Time (NPT) | Place | Observation | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8:20 am | Rasuwagadhi border area, Tibet | Magnitude 2.6 tremor recorded. | The Week |
| 8:37 am | Rasuwagadhi border area, Tibet | Putative magnitude 4.4 "earthquake" recorded; later re-catalogued as a magnitude 5.2 glacial collapse. | Wikipedia timeline (uncited in wikitext) |
| 8:40 am | Syabrubesi, Rasuwa district | Water-level monitoring station records the Trishuli River (locally called Bhotekoshi River here) at 1.62 m. Warning level 6 m, danger level 9 m. | Kathmandu Post ("How the Bhotekoshi flood unfolded over 10 hours") |
| 8:44 am | Gyirong Port, Xigazê prefecture, Tibet | CCTV captures the start of the destruction of Gyirong Port. Debris flow covered ~20 km in ~7 minutes (~180 km/h), aided by a drop in elevation of nearly 3,000 m. | Xigazê prefecture government verification; CCTV; Kathmandu Post |
| 8:50 am | Syabrubesi | Station ceases transmission; last reading (8:40) was 1.62 m. | ReliefWeb (NPL: Flood – 08-2026 – Rasuwa Flood) |
| ~8:50–9:00 am | Rasuwagadhi customs complex / Friendship Bridge, border | Surge reaches the customs complex and Friendship Bridge, sweeping both away along with three Nepal Police posts (Timure, Rasuwagadhi, Syabrubesi). | ReliefWeb; The Week |
| 9:00–9:05 am | Rasuwa District Administration Office / Betrabati hydrological station | FFD learns a major flood entered the Bhotekoshi River from Tibet; surge enters the Trishuli River and spreads more gradually from here. | Onlinekhabar ("Technical report details Bhotekoshi flood's journey from Tibet to Devghat") |
| 9:15–9:16 am | Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Chitwan districts | SMS emergency alerts sent to 679,295 riverbank residents; authorities begin evacuating/warning people along the Trishuli corridor up to Muglin. | Onlinekhabar |
| 9:20 am | Betrawati | Monitoring station ceases transmission. Last recorded level 3.55 m (warning 4.1 m, danger 5 m). | Onlinekhabar |
| 10:28 am | Dhading–Galchhi area | Flood forecast to reach this area and pass Muglin within a few hours; Prithvi Highway, Muglin–Narayanghat road and Trishuli-bank settlements alerted. | Kathmandu Post |
| 11:26 am | Phurke (Malekhu) | Monitoring station records the Trishuli River above the danger mark. | Kathmandu Post |
| 11:43 am | Phurke / Malekhu | Phurke river bridge and other structures, plus the Malekhu monitoring station, swept away. | Onlinekhabar |
| 11:50 am | Malekhu | Area suffers impact. | Wikipedia timeline (uncited in wikitext) |
| 1:00 pm | Muglin → Devghat | Authorities confirm the flood has passed Muglin; riverine residents of the Trishuli as far as Devghat alerted. | Kathmandu Post |
| 1:30 pm | Devghat | Water level reaches 4.76 m. Main surge expected ~3:30 pm, with a potential rise to 8 m calculated. | Kathmandu Post |
| 1:35 pm | — | Authorities again warn the China-side barrier lake had not fully drained and risk remained. | Onlinekhabar |
| 2:14 pm | Kalikhola | Monitoring station reaches danger level; peak level 12.3 m. | Kathmandu Post |
| 3:20 pm | Narayani River – Devghat area | Flood arrives; level still rising. | Wikipedia timeline (uncited in wikitext) |
| 4:00 pm | Devghat | Water level peaks at 6.57 m, begins to fall. | Wikipedia timeline (uncited in wikitext) |
| 6:13 pm (14:28 UTC+2) | — | International Charter Space and Major Disasters activated at the request of Nepal's DHM; UNITAR designated managing agency. | disasterscharter.org |
| 6:30 pm | Devghat | Water level falls back to ~4 m. Flood estimated to have carried an additional ~20 million m³ of water through Devghat. ~10 hours elapsed since the seismic signal. Four automatic hydrological stations destroyed (Syabrubesi, Rasuwa–Bhotekoshi, Betrawati, Malekhu/Phurke). | Onlinekhabar |

Notes on the table:

- "Bhotekoshi River" as used for the Syabrubesi/Rasuwa reach in these sources
  is the Wikipedia article's naming choice; it flags this explicitly as
  distinct from the better-known Bhote Koshi (a Sun Koshi tributary further
  east), which was not affected.
- Two entries (11:50 am Malekhu impact; 3:20 pm and 4:00 pm Devghat) carry no
  inline citation in the fetched Wikipedia wikitext — flag these for extra
  scrutiny against the FFD report before use.
- Response/relief-effort entries (military deployment, helicopter counts, road
  and hydropower damage, IFRC funding, etc.) from the same "26 August" section
  are omitted here since this doc is scoped to the gauge/flood-front sequence.

the timeline narrative and changes daily; NDRRMA publishes a Nepali situation
